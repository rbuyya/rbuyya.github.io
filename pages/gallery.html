Top 10 Shringar Darshan

Experience the divine beauty of the Deities as they are beautifully decorated in ten distinct traditional attires (Beshas) throughout the calendar year. Swipe or scroll horizontally to see them all.

1 / 10

Mangala Besha

The pristine dawn awakening look. The Deities are elegantly wrapped in fine cotton fabrics, creating a serene and deeply moving atmosphere for early-morning pilgrims.

2 / 10

Abakasha Darshan

The daily morning bathing attire. Tailored beautifully for the sacred tooth-brushing, holy water ablutions, and daily spiritual purification rituals.

3 / 10

Bada Singhara Besha

The magnificent night-time sleeping attire. Rich local blossoms, fresh jasmines, and handwoven silks containing sacred verses are arranged lovingly around the Deities.

4 / 10

Suna Besha

A glorious, highly anticipated appearance where the Deities are fully adorned with heavy, sparkling golden ornaments on the grand chariots.

5 / 10

Gajanana Besha

Celebrated during Snana Purnima. The Deities are beautifully dressed in an elegant elephant mask structure, delighting devotees of Lord Ganesha.

6 / 10

Govardhana Besha

A beautiful arrangement that tells the ancient story of Lord Krishna lifting the massive Govardhan Hill to keep his pure devotees safe.

7 / 10

Padma Besha

A brilliant, soothing dress arrangement decorated with beautiful lotus designs, symbolizing pure devotion and absolute spiritual cleanliness.

8 / 10

Nabagrah Besha

A highly symbolic alignment representing protection and cosmic shelter, assuring all visiting souls that the Supreme Lord guards their journeys.

9 / 10

Sadabhishekh

A magnificent royal look put together after large-scale festival cleanings, utilizing highly exquisite silks and selective classic ornaments.

10 / 10

Chandan Besha

An elegant, high-summer cooling relief arrangement. The Deities are gently coated with natural, hand-ground cooling sandalwood paste.

Spiritual Leaders

Honoring the revered spiritual guides, teachers, and acharyas who have graced our temple premises in Bidar with their holy footprints and transcendental discourses.

HG Sahadeva Das

Shiksha Guru

An exemplary spiritual educator who consistently delivers deep scriptural insights to the congregation. His foundational guidance structures our ongoing community classes, helping local families integrate Vedic concepts into everyday life.

HH Mahaman Guru Maharaj

Deeksha Guru

A deeply revered initiating master whose powerful spiritual focus has connected hundreds of local seekers to formal devotional shelter. His occasional temple visits always feature sublime, heart-transforming initiation fires and traditional assemblies.

HG Shravan Bhakt Prabhuji

Senior Preacher & Kirtan Guide

Widely celebrated for his absolute dedication to congregational development. He consistently visits Bidar to head large-scale community kirtan gatherings, illuminating how sacred sound vibration helps heal structural social stress.

HG Amogh Leela Prabhuji

Renowned Motivational Speaker

A widely celebrated lifestyle counselor and modern monk who explains ancient scriptural facts using delightful real-world humor and everyday logic. His massive public assemblies in Bidar draw huge crowds of young professionals and students.

HG Radhika Kripa Devi Dasi

Bhakti Discourse Speaker

A deeply loved speaker who fills the hearts of local audiences with sweet, deeply emotional discourses on pure Bhakti philosophy. Her classes focus on internal meditative steps and household peace.

HH Kinkar Damodar Maharaj

Traveling Sannyasi & Scholar

An ascetic whose profound lifestyle and detailed scriptural analysis serve as a beautiful inspiration. His lectures provide deep cross-references across multiple classic standard text compilations.

HG Sachi Suta Das

Youth Outreach Coordinator

A high-energy counselor focused completely on directing youth talent toward community support and clean values. His seminars regularly clarify practical career challenges through ethical lenses.
