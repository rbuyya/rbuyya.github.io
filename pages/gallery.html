1. Types of Shringar Darshan

The visual logs of the deep transcendental transformations and ceremonial decorations of the Deities.

Mangala Besha

The early dawn awakening attire. The Deities are dressed gently in soft, immaculate cotton cloths called Khandua fabrics, bringing peace to early morning pilgrims.

Abakasha Darshan

The morning bathing dress arrangement. Tailored specifically for the regular sacred tooth-brushing, water ablation, and pure cleansing rituals of the Lord.

Bada Singhara Besha

The highly artistic evening sleeping attire. The Deities are beautifully garlanded in rich local blossoms, fragrant jasmines, and special handwoven silks inscribed with sacred verses.

Suna Besha (Regal Gold)

A mesmerizing occasional appearance where Lord Jagannath, Balabhadra, and Subhadra Devi are dressed completely in beautiful, heavy solid gold ornaments on special high-festive dates.

Gajanana / Hathi Besha

Celebrated beautifully on the afternoon of Snana Purnima. The holy deities are intricately adorned with elephant masks and attire, echoing the beloved appearance of Lord Ganesha.

Giri Govardhana Besha

A beautiful seasonal special arrangement depicting the transcendental pastimes of Lord Krishna lifting the massive Govardhan Hill to protect the resident devotees.
