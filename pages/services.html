Our Services

Spiritual Services for All Devotees

Daily Services & Rituals

Darshan (Divine Viewing)

Receive the divine blessings of Lord Jagannatha. Our daily darshan service allows devotees to seek spiritual enlightenment and receive the grace of the deity. Please note the temple remains closed for afternoon hours.

Morning to Afternoon: 5:00 AM - 1:00 PM

Afternoon Closure: 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM

Evening: 4:00 PM - 8:30 PM

Mangala Aarti

The auspicious first aarti of the day performed early in the morning to awaken the deities and start the day with powerful spiritual vibrations.

Time: 5:00 AM - 5:30 AM

Shringar Aarti

Performed after the deities are adorned in their beautiful daily attire, fresh garments, and sacred flower garlands.

Time: 7:00 AM - 7:30 AM

Raj Bhoga Aarti

A grand midday ritual held accompanying the main food offerings (Bhoga) presented to the supreme Lord.

Time: 12:30 PM - 1:00 PM

Dhoop Aarti

An afternoon incense ritual symbolizing purification and deep dedication as the evening hours commence.

Time: 4:00 PM - 4:15 PM

Gaura Aarti

The beautiful evening congregational kirtan and light ceremony, drawing the community together in prayer and devotion.

Time: 7:00 PM - 7:30 PM

Weekly Feast

Sunday Special Evening Program

Join us every Sunday evening for an immersive spiritual experience and association with devotees.

Ecstatic Kirtan

Congregational chanting of the holy names to awaken spiritual consciousness and bliss.

Tulsi Puja

Sacred worship and circumambulation of Srimati Tulsi Devi accompanied by traditional prayers.

Gauranga Aarti

A grand evening aarti ceremony filled with beautiful lamps, incense, and grand kirtan celebrations.

Bhagavad Gita & Srimad Bhagavatam Satsang

Enlightening discourses and scriptural wisdom from the sacred texts, guiding us in practical spiritual life.

Free Mahaprasadam Feast

A sanctified, delicious vegetarian feast lovingly prepared and served to all visiting devotees.

Special Services

Archana Seva

Personalized offering chanting the holy 108 names of the Lord along with Name and Gotra sankalpa blessings.

Vehicle Pooja

Sacred blessings and protection rituals for your new two-wheelers and four-wheelers to assure safe journeys.

Raj Bhog Seva

Sponsor a majestic royal midday bhog offering featuring traditional multi-course preparations for the deities.

Nitya Seva

Continuous daily devotional dedication encompassing five daily food offerings and exclusive prayer services.

Ekadashi Seva

Special fast-day offerings featuring grain-free bhoga and deepadaan ceremonies to enhance spiritual progress.

Annadana Seva

Participate in the ultimate charity by sponsoring sacred prasadam food distribution programs for devotees.

Spiritual Programs & Events

01

Bhagavat Classes

Enlightening interactive scriptural studies customized separately for both adults and children to nurture values and spiritual roots:

  • Adult Classes: Every Tuesday and Friday from 7:30 PM to 8:30 PM.
  • Children Classes: Held every Sunday evening, offering a well-known Free Value Education program.
02

Festival Celebrations

Join the community during grand celebrations observed in the following sequence:

  • Pranpratishta Deity Anniversary Celebration
  • Gaura Purnima Mahotsva
  • Maha Shivaratri
  • Raam Navami Mahotsva
  • Narasimha Jayanti Mahotsva
  • Ratha Yatra
  • Krishna Janmashtmi Mahotsva
  • Radhastmi Mahotsva
  • Govardhan Mahotsva
  • Gopashtami Mahotsva
  • Tulsi Vivah Celebration
03

Gau Seva

Connect with our cosmic mother at the temple gaushala. Devotees can feed green grass, fresh jaggery, and assist in caring for our cows.

04

Scriptural Teachings

Regular public lectures dissecting complex Vedic philosophies like the Bhagavad Gita and Srimad Bhagavatam into simple, actionable steps.

05

Youth Programs

Targeted youth dynamic circles focusing on character development, mental clarity, modern lifestyle stress control, and cultural grounding.
