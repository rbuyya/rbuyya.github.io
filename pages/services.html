Sunday Special

Mega Evening Feast & Satsang Program

Join us every Sunday evening with your family to experience a transformative spiritual gathering full of devotion, knowledge, and holy offerings.

Ecstatic Kirtan

Congregational chanting of holy names to purify the heart and awaken spiritual joy.

Tulsi Pooja

Sacred worship and circumambulation of Srimati Tulsi Devi for devotional blessings.

Gauranga Aarti

Grand evening aarti ceremony with intense kirtan, lamps, and beautiful flowers.

Bhagavad Gita Discourse

Insightful, practical wisdom from the timeless verses of the Gita applied to daily life.

Srimad Bhagavatam Satsang

Deep dive into the pastimes and philosophical glories of the Supreme Lord.

Jagannath Prabhu Mahaprasad

Honoring sanctified, purely vegetarian multi-course feast offered to Jagannath and given to all devotees with blessings of Lord Prabhu.

Spiritual Programs & Events

01

Festival Celebrations

Celebrate major Hindu festivals including Gauranga Appearance, Raam Navami, Narsimha Appearance, Rathayatra, Krishna Janmashtami, Govarhan Festival, Radhashtami, Karthik Damodar Season, Tulsi Vivaaha along with other sacred occasions with grand celebrations and community gatherings.

02

Gau Seva

Engage in the sacred service of protecting and nurturing cows. Devotees can participate in feeding, sheltering, and caring for temple cows to receive boundless spiritual blessings and peace.

03

Scriptural Teachings

Participate in regular discourses on sacred texts like Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, and other Hindu philosophical texts explained in simple terms.

04

Community Service

Participate in our social service programs including food distribution seva, Temple Decoration seva during festive times, and voluntary seva to serve society and the divine.

05

Youth Programs

Special programs designed for youth to connect with spirituality, learn cultural values, and develop character through temple activities.
