Frequently Asked Questions

What are the temple visiting hours?

The temple is open from 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Morning hours are 6:00 AM to 12:00 PM, afternoon from 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM, and evening from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Special hours may apply during festivals.

How do I book a puja or special service?

You can book services by calling us, emailing us, or visiting the temple in person. Use our contact form above to send a message, and our team will get back to you within 24 hours with booking details and pricing information.

Is there an entry fee to visit the temple?

No, there is no entry fee to visit the temple. Entry is free for all devotees. However, donations (dakshina) are welcomed to support temple maintenance and community programs.

Can I sponsor temple programs or events?

Yes, we welcome sponsorships and donations for various temple programs and events. Please contact us for details on sponsorship opportunities and how you can contribute.

Do you offer spiritual counseling?

Yes, we provide spiritual guidance and counseling sessions based on Hindu philosophy and ancient scriptures. Schedule a session with our experienced spiritual mentors by contacting us.

How can I volunteer at the temple?

We welcome volunteers to support our temple activities and community service programs. Contact us with your interests and availability, and we'll provide opportunities that match your skills.
